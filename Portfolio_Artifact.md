## What This Project Says About Me as an AI Engineer

This project shows I prioritize clarity and trust over impressive metrics. I built a system that's easy to understand, thoroughly tested, and transparent about how it works. I built a system where every recommendation can be traced back to a specific reason, where guardrails prevent misuse before it happens, and where the trade-offs are documented honestly. I discovered early on that the hardest part of building AI systems isn't the algorithm—it's being thoughtful about what you're optimizing for and having the courage to reject a "better" solution if it sacrifices transparency. This project shows that I care about building systems people can trust, not systems that maximize metrics at the expense of understanding.
